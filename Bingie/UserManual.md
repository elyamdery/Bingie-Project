## Getting Started

### Installation

1. Download the Bingie app from your device's app store
2. Open the app after installation is complete

### Creating an Account

1. On the login screen, tap "Register"
2. Enter a username and password
3. Tap "Register" to create your account
4. You will be automatically logged in after successful registration

### Logging In

1. Enter your username and password
2. Check "Remember Me" if you want to stay logged in
3. Tap "Login" to access your account

## Home Screen

The Home screen is your main dashboard for tracking binge episodes.

### Features

- **Current Time**: Displays the current time at the top of the screen
- **"I Binged" Button**: Tap this button whenever you experience a binge episode
- **Binge Counter**: Shows the number of binges recorded for the current day
- **Visual Tracker**: Red dots appear below the button to visually represent each binge episode

### How to Record a Binge

1. When you experience a binge episode, open the app
2. Tap the large red "I Binged" button
3. The counter will increment, and a red dot will appear in the tracker

## History Screen

The History screen allows you to view your binge episodes over time.

### Features

- **Weekly Calendar**: Shows a week-by-week view of your binge episodes
- **Navigation Controls**: Use the arrows to move between weeks
- **Day Details**: Tap on any day to see detailed statistics

### Viewing Day Statistics

1. Navigate to the week containing the day you want to view
2. Tap on the specific day
3. View the number of binges recorded for that day
4. Tap "Back to History" to return to the calendar view

## Explore Screen

The Explore screen provides resources and community features to support your recovery.

### Features

- **Daily Motivation**: Inspirational quotes to keep you motivated
- **Leaderboard**: See how your recovery compares to others in the community
- **Articles**: Educational content about binge eating disorder and recovery strategies

### Reading Articles

1. Scroll down to the Articles section
2. Tap on an article title that interests you
3. Read the full article content
4. Tap "Back to Explore" to return to the main Explore screen

## Account Management

### Changing Your Password

Currently, password changes are not supported in the app. Please contact support if you need to reset your password.

### Logging Out

1. Close the app to log out
2. If you checked "Remember Me" during login, you will be automatically logged in next time

## Tips for Success

- **Consistent Tracking**: Record all binge episodes, even if it's difficult
- **Review Your History**: Look for patterns in your binge behavior
- **Read the Articles**: Learn about binge eating disorder to better understand your condition
- **Stay Motivated**: Use the daily quotes for inspiration
- **Community Support**: Compare your progress with others on the leaderboard

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Issue**: App crashes when recording a binge
**Solution**: Ensure your device has sufficient storage and is running the latest OS version

**Issue**: Cannot log in despite correct credentials
**Solution**: Check your internet connection and try again

**Issue**: Data not showing in History
**Solution**: Make sure you've recorded binges on those days

### Contact Support

If you encounter any issues not covered in this manual, please contact our support team at support@bingie.app.
